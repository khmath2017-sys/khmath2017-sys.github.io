Professional Profile

Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences (Ph.D. in Differential Equations) and Associate Professor (Docent) with over 17 years of international academic, research, and administrative experience. Proven track record in higher education leadership as Head of Department and Vice Dean for Academic Affairs. Specialized in pre-university preparation, adapting advanced mathematical courses for international students in Russian and English, and aligning curricula with international accreditation standards (ABET / NCAAA). Actively engaged in mathematical modeling, Stepanov spaces, and computational mathematics using Python and Lean 4.
